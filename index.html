Claude on my Mac

A personal tool that lets one teacher's own computer read her own Google Drive and Calendar.

This page exists because Google asks every application that signs in with a Google account to publish a home page, a privacy policy and terms of use. This is that home page.

What the application is

It is a small program that runs on one personal Mac. It is not a website, not a service and not a product. Nobody can sign up for it. It has one user, who is also the person who wrote it, and it is used to help organise her own teaching files and her own calendar.

What it does

  • Lists and reads files in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Uploads files from the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Reads the owner's own Google Calendar.

What it does not do

  • It has no server. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google.
  • It shows no adverts, collects no analytics and builds no profile.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It is not used with anyone else's Google account.

Source

The code runs locally and is not distributed. Questions can be raised at github.com/Nimo-star.
